How it works

  1. Register and pay the annual membership fee.
  2. Once approved, you get your own member ID (e.g. VML0001).
  3. After any 4-player game, one player logs everyone's score.
  4. The other 3 players log in and confirm the result.
  5. Once all 3 confirm, the leaderboard updates automatically.
Anyone can view the leaderboard. Only approved members can log or confirm matches.
