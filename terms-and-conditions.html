Membership

Membership is open to individuals who register and pay the annual membership fee of ₹1000. Membership is personal and non-transferable, and is valid for 12 months from the date of approval or renewal. New registrations are subject to admin approval before becoming active.

Renewal

Membership must be renewed annually (₹1000) to remain active. A lapsed (unrenewed) membership is hidden from the public leaderboard until renewed; your existing points and match history are preserved and reappear once you renew.

Match logging & scoring

Any active member may log a 4-player match, entering all 4 players' member IDs and scores. A match only counts toward the leaderboard once all 3 other players confirm it. Raw scores must sum to the fixed pool total for the match category. Members are expected to report scores honestly; disputed matches can be rejected by any of the 3 confirming players instead of confirmed.
